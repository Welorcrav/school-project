Main Rules

5 Friendship Rules

Simple rules for a strong friendship.

Rule 1

Listen to your friend

Always listen carefully when your friend is speaking to you.

Rule 2

Always be honest

Do not lie to your friend. Trust is very important.

Rule 3

Respect each other

Respect your friend's personal space and choices.

Rule 4

Help your friend

Support your friend when they have problems or hard times.

Rule 5

Keep secrets safe

Never tell your friend's secrets to other people.

Useful Tips

3 Good Tips

How to be the best friend.

🎉

Be happy for their success

Always be glad when your friend wins or does something great.

🤝

Say sorry when you are wrong

Saying sorry quickly fixes small fights and saves friendship.

⏳

Spend time together

Play games, talk, and spend free time with your friend.
